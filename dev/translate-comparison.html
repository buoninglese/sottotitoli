Recommendation
Keep Google as primary, Opus-MT as free fallback. Google is fastest and most reliable. Opus-MT is $0 and nearly as good for EN↔IT — perfect as a fallback if Google has issues. NLLB only becomes worth it if you need 200-language support or complete data privacy (your own endpoint). The current setup (Google + Opus-MT via provider: "hf") gives you speed + zero-cost redundancy. If Google costs become significant, you can swap priority: make Opus-MT the default and keep Google as paid fallback.
