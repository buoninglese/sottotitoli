# brand-voice.md — Sottotitoli Brand Voice

> **For any AI agent creating copy, marketing, or UI text for Sottotitoli.**
> The brand voice is the personality of the product. Stay consistent with it.

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Sottotitoli** (Italian for "Subtitles") is a real-time AI captioning and translation tool positioned at the intersection of **comprehension and learning**. The tagline on the landing page says it all:

> *"Ascolta in inglese. Leggi in italiano. Capisci tutto, subito."*
> (Listen in English. Read in Italian. Understand everything, instantly.)

The website is **www.sottotitoli.pro**.

---

## 2. Tone Pillars


### 🧠 Intelligent but Warm
This is an AI tool, but it's not cold or robotic. It's a companion for language learners. Think "brilliant friend who explains things clearly" rather than "corporate software."

### 🇮🇹 Proudly Italian
The UI is entirely in Italian. The brand doesn't apologize for being Italian — it embraces it. 

### ✨ Aspirational
The brand sells a better future — understanding a new language, connecting across cultures. The tone is optimistic, forward-looking, but not hype-y.

---

## 3. Language Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| **UI in Italian always** | "Avvia sessione" not "Start Session" |
| **Code/comments in English** | `// Main application logic` |
| **Headlines: short, rhythmic** | "Capisci tutto, subito." |
| **Avoid corporate jargon** | Never say "leverage synergistic solutions" |
| **Use the second person (tu)** | "Il tuo viaggio" not "Il viaggio dell'utente" |
| **No exclamation marks in UI** | "Sessione salvata" not "Sessione salvata!" |
| **Calls to action: verb-first** | "Avvia sessione", "Acquista crediti", "Scarica report" |

---

## 4. Key Messaging Themes

### The Promise
> Real-time captions + translation that helps you learn, not just read.

### The Problem
> You're talking with someone in a language you're learning. You miss words. You fall behind. You give up.

### The Solution
> Live captions that appear as people speak. Translation side-by-side. Vocabulary tracking. Grammar feedback. All in one screen.

### The Differentiator
> It's not just subtitles — it's a learning environment disguised as a caption tool.

## 6. Visual Voice (for designers)

While this is a copy document, the visual voice informs the language:

- **Color palette:** Cyan/teal accents on dark backgrounds (night). Warm cyan and purples on light backgrounds (day).
- **Typography:** Inter (UI body), Manrope (headlines), JetBrains Mono (labels/eyebrow text), Cormorant Garamond (hero serif accents on panoramica).
- **Spacing:** Air between elements. Never cramped.
- **Motion:** Smooth cubic-bezier transitions. Nothing jarring.

---

## 7. What We Never Sound Like

- ❌ A tech startup ("disrupting the language learning space!")
- ❌ A corporate LMS ("module-based adaptive learning framework")
- ❌ A generic translation app ("Translate any language instantly!")
- ❌ Overly casual/playful (no emoji in UI copy unless it's an icon)
- ❌ Academic/clinical ("lexical diversity metrics")
