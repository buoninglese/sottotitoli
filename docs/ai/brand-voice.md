## 1. Brand Identity

**Sottotitoli** = Real-time AI captioning + translation. Positioned at the intersection of **comprehension and learning**.

> *"Ascolta in italiano. Leggi in inglese. Capisci tutto, subito."*

---

## 2. Tone Pillars

### 🎬 Cinematic
Wes Anderson's meticulous composition. Tarantino's bold confidence. Language should feel deliberate, atmospheric.

### 🧠 Intelligent but Warm
A brilliant friend who explains things clearly. Not corporate software.

### 🇮🇹 Proudly Italian
UI entirely in Italian. Legal: "Legge italiana," "Tribunali di Bari." Sense of place.

### ✨ Aspirational
Sells a better future — understanding a new language, connecting across cultures.

---

## 3. Language Rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| UI in Italian always | "Avvia sessione" not "Start Session" |
| Code/comments in English | `// Main application logic` |
| Headlines: short, rhythmic | "Capisci tutto, subito." |
| No corporate jargon | Never "leverage synergistic solutions" |
| Second person (tu) | "Il tuo viaggio" |
| No exclamation marks in UI | "Sessione salvata" |
| CTA: verb-first | "Avvia sessione," "Acquista crediti" |

---

## 4. Product Naming

| Term | Usage |
|------|-------|
| **Sottotitoli** | Full product name. Don't abbreviate. |
| **sottotitoli.pro** | Premium tier. Always lowercase. |
| **Voice Credits (VC)** | In-app currency. |
| **Panoramica** | Dashboard page. |
| **DUO+** | Multi-speaker mode. |

---

## 5. What We Never Sound Like

- ❌ Tech startup ("disrupting the space!")
- ❌ Corporate LMS ("module-based framework")
- ❌ Generic translator ("Translate instantly!")
- ❌ Overly casual (no emoji in UI copy)

---
