## Core Anti-Goals

These are the paths I refuse, no matter how attractive they appear:

1. **Trading time for money.** I build assets, not hours. If income stops when I stop working, it's not a business — it's a job.

2. **Being the bottleneck.** If a system requires me to function, it's fragile. I design for my absence.

3. **Low-margin work.** Any project that pays less than my hourly threshold or doesn't compound is a distraction, not an opportunity.

4. **Vendor dependency.** I do not build my income on platforms I don't control. APIs can deprecate. Algorithms change. My value must be portable.

5. **Neglecting date night / family presence.** Wealth without presence is poverty in disguise. The system must protect family time, not consume it.

6. **Employee mindset.** I am not waiting for permission, a promotion, or a boss's approval. I create value directly.

7. **Complexity addiction.** Building something clever that nobody needs. Complexity is a tax; simplicity is leverage.

8. **Reactivity.** Responding to inbound requests without strategic filtering. The Anti-Goal Firewall exists to say NO.

9. **Perfectionism as procrastination.** "Polishing" instead of shipping. Done > perfect. The Decision Forge tracks output, not intention.

10. **Solo hero syndrome.** Not building with others, not delegating, not asking for help. Wealth is a team sport.
