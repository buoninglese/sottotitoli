Risk Register

1. Free tier cliffs
Supabase free→Pro at 500MB DB (~$25/mo). Render hobby→standard (~$25/mo). Combined ~$50/mo jump at scale.
2. Prepaid = zero recurring revenue
No MRR predictability. A user buying once may never return. Consider hybrid subscription + top-up model.
3. MyMemory ceiling
Free tier may throttle at volume. Fallback: Google Translate (free) or GPT-4o-mini (€0.006/min).
4. OpenAI dependency (AI reports only)
Live transcription uses free browser Web Speech API. OpenAI is only for AI report generation. If pricing changes, reports get more expensive but captions keep working. Switch to GPT-4o-mini for 10× cheaper reports if needed.
5. Forfettario €85k cap
Revenue above €85k forces ordinario regime (~35%+ effective rate). Track monthly.

Recommendations

1. Add Stripe fees (~2%) to cost stack — currently missing.
2. Test a hybrid model: €4.99/mo sub for 60min + top-up packs for heavy users. Gives MRR + upsell.
3. At €0.033/min you could raise Premium to €44.99 and still be cheapest in class.
4. The language-learning angle (CEFR, vocabulary, AI reports) is your moat. Lean into it.
5. Monitor Supabase/Render usage monthly — the free→paid jump is the biggest margin shock.
