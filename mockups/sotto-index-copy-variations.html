🗣️ Tone of Voice — The "Teacher From Salento" Voice

  • Warm, not cold. This is a person who's spent 5 years in a classroom, not a SaaS dashboard.
  • Specific, not generic. "Phrasal verbs," "false friends," "CEFR B1" — use real teaching language. It builds credibility.
  • Honest, not hype. No "rivoluzionare." No "il primo." If the product is good, the truth is enough.
  • Italian rhythm, English precision. Italian copy should flow with the cadence of spoken Italian. Short sentences. Punchy ends.
  • "Noi" over "io." The brand voice is collective — "noi italiani," "chi impara." Save first-person for the About page.
  • Confident but not arrogant. The teacher KNOWS this works. Say it with conviction. No "se vuoi," no "può diventare."
  • Emotional but not sentimental. "Chiaro." is emotional because it's true, not because it's dressed up. Same for all copy.
  • The demo is the star. Slides 1 and 2 have working interactive demos. Let them do the heavy lifting. Copy supports, doesn't shout over them.

🔭 Holistic Assessment — The Story Across 5 Slides

The current problem: Each slide was written in isolation. Slide 0 claims revolution. Slide 2 has an English tag. Slide 3 hedges. Slide 4 repeats the "capire" theme instead of closing the deal. There's no narrative thread, and the founder's teaching experience — the entire reason this product EXISTS — is invisible.

The fix: A single story across 5 moves: (0) You struggle to understand English in real time → (1) Here's what instant clarity feels like → (2) One tap unlocks everything about any word, without stopping → (3) Every session builds a picture of your progress, like a private teacher would → (4) Fair, honest pricing. Start free.

The untouchables are correct. "Tutto chiaro. Sempre. Subito." and "Chiaro." are the emotional anchors. Everything else serves them.

(4) Inject the teacher's perspective into at least one paragraph — slide 0 or slide 3 are the natural homes for it.
