Scroll Indicators

10 scroll animation indicator concepts, each shown in a card. All animated with CSS.

Classic triple chevron. Subtle, used on the hero.

#2 · Pulsing Ring

Circle inhales/exhales. Good for minimal dark hero sections.

#3 · Progress Bar

Vertical fill bar slides down. Suggests "scroll to fill".

#4 · Mouse Wheel

Literal mouse icon. Universal "scroll down" symbol.

#5 · Fading Dots

Three dots fade in sequence. Minimal and elegant.

#6 · Spinning Arrow

Arrow spins in a circle. High-energy, tech-forward.

#7 · Breathing Glow

Pulsing purple orb. Dreamy, modern, SaaS feel.

#8 · Finger Swipe

#9 · Elastic Line

Stretchy pill slides back and forth. Playful physics.
