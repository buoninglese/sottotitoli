📋 Key insight

White text on transparent background looks grey because the page background (~#0f1117) shows through, effectively blending the white text with dark grey. To keep subtitles bright white, you need either: (a) a solid or semi-transparent dark bar behind the text, or (b) a thick text stroke that acts as a local background per glyph. The NLTV approach (Var 7) uses stroke but paint-order: stroke fill paints the fill on top, keeping the white bright while the black outline frames each letter.
