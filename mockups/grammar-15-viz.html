9. Radar Chart — Language Dimensions

5 axes: Vocabulary, Grammar, Fluency, Accuracy, Complexity
Each axis = 0-100 normalized score

accuracy = 100 - (repeatedWords / total × 100)
complexity = avgWordLength / 12 × 100
