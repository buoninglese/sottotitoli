Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Sottotitoli collects only the minimum data necessary to provide real-time captioning and translation services:

  • Account data: Email address and name from Google Sign-In
  • Audio data: Microphone input is processed locally in your browser for speech recognition. Audio is streamed to our speech-to-text provider and is not stored
  • Transcript data: Captured text from your sessions is stored for your review. Unsaved sessions are automatically and permanently deleted after 30 days. Sessions you explicitly save are kept until you delete them yourself.
  • Usage data: Session duration, word count, and language preferences to provide analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide real-time captions and translations during your sessions
  • To generate personal analytics (WPM, vocabulary size, grammar scores)
  • To save your word bank and preferences across sessions
  • To improve service quality and fix bugs

We never sell your data. We never use your speech content for advertising.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

Your data is stored on Supabase servers in the EU (Frankfurt region).

Automatic cleanup: A scheduled job runs daily and permanently deletes any session that is not marked as "saved" and is older than 30 days. This is fully automated — no action needed from you.

Self-serve deletion: You can delete any of your sessions, reports, or your entire account at any time directly from the app — no need to contact anyone. Go to Trascrizioni to delete individual sessions, or Account → Privacy & Dati to delete your entire account. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication, database, and storage (EU-hosted)
  • Google Web Speech API: Browser-based speech recognition (audio processed locally)
  • LanguageTool API: Grammar checking for analysis panels

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR (EU) and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you
  • Correct or delete your data at any time — directly from the app, no approval needed
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format

For questions about this policy, contact us at studiobuoninglese@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

Sottotitoli uses only essential cookies for authentication (Supabase session token). No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

7. Children

Sottotitoli is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
