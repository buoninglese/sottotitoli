Choose the right operational surface before you start. Live Caption is for real-time overlay and transcript publishing. Translator is for spoken translation workflows without the caption-specific clutter.

Live Caption

Use this when you want a clean real-time caption workspace with a clear microphone signal, final caption feed, room-based overlay publishing, and session reporting support.

  • Prominent Interim mic feedback.
  • Overlay room workflow and shareable overlay link.
  • Transcript capture plus lesson and speaker reporting flow.

Translator

  • No caption-first overlay controls in the main interface.
